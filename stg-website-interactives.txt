MASTER BUILD PROMPT

Build the “Hold This Today” Scripture Content and Distribution System for Simplify to Glorify

You are working inside the existing Simplify to Glorify website project.

Your task is to design and build a complete, production-minded content system called:

Hold This Today

Working tagline: One Scripture, one gentle encouragement, one prayer, and one small step for today.

This must be more than a visual daily devotional page. Build an interconnected system that can use one approved master content entry to power:





A daily website feature



Permanent, indexable website pages



A topic-based encouragement library



Pinterest-ready graphics and Pin metadata



An email subscription funnel



Automated or scheduled email sequences



Topic-specific subscriber journeys



Related-resource recommendations



Content scheduling and publishing



Long-term reuse of evergreen content

The system should help women discover meaningful Scripture-centered encouragement, save it, share it, subscribe to receive more, and naturally explore relevant Simplify to Glorify resources.

1. PRIMARY GOAL

Create a calm, trustworthy, Scripture-centered experience that becomes a repeat destination for women who are:





Anxious



Grieving



Caregiving



Exhausted



Waiting



Struggling with regret



Living with chronic pain



Feeling discouraged



Learning to pray



Trying to trust God



Feeling far from God



Carrying more than they can easily explain

The visitor should be able to arrive from Pinterest, search, email, or another website page and quickly receive something genuinely useful.

The experience should communicate:

You do not have to solve everything today. Here is one truth from Scripture to hold onto right now.

Do not build a generic quote generator, shallow inspirational feed, personality quiz, gamified devotional app, or AI-generated spiritual advice tool.

2. CORE CONTENT PHILOSOPHY

All published content must come from a reviewed and approved source of truth.

The application must never:





Invent Scripture



Paraphrase Scripture without explicit approval



Change a supplied Bible verse



Combine unrelated verses



Generate theology independently



Automatically publish unreviewed AI-generated spiritual content



Diagnose mental-health conditions



Promise healing or emotional resolution



Suggest that one activity will fix a difficult circumstance



Pressure visitors to feel better, forgive quickly, stop grieving, or reach a spiritual conclusion

The system may format, organize, schedule, filter, distribute, and display approved content.

It must not become the theological author.

Default Scripture translation is:

NASB 2020

Every Scripture entry must retain its supplied:





Reference



Exact Scripture text



Translation label



Verification status
